## 2. Step-by-Step Guide to Upload and Submit on Kaggle

### Step 1: Open Your Kaggle Competition Page
1. Log in to your registered Kaggle account.
2. Navigate to your competition page for the IITM MLP project.
3. Click on the **Code** tab.

### Step 2: Open or Upload Your Notebook
- **If you already have the registered notebook created:**
  1. Open your existing notebook (`24f2006569-notebook-2026t3`).
  2. Click **File > Upload Notebook** and select [`24f2006569-notebook-2026t3.ipynb`](file:///c:/Users/acer/Documents/IITM%20BS%20MLP/antigravity_project/24f2006569-notebook-2026t3.ipynb).
- **If creating fresh:**
  1. Click **New Notebook**.
  2. In the top-left title, rename the notebook to exactly: `24f2006569-notebook-2026t3`.
  3. Click **File > Upload Notebook** and upload [`24f2006569-notebook-2026t3.ipynb`](file:///c:/Users/acer/Documents/IITM%20BS%20MLP/antigravity_project/24f2006569-notebook-2026t3.ipynb).

### Step 3: Verify Collaborator & Privacy Settings
1. In the top-right toolbar of the Kaggle notebook, click **Share**.
2. Under **Visibility**, ensure it is set to **Private**.
3. Under **Collaborators**, add `iitmbscs2008p` and give **Viewer** access.
4. Click **Save**.

### Step 4: Run & Commit the Notebook
1. In the top-right toolbar, click the **Save Version** button.
2. Select **Version type: Save & Run All (Commit)**.
3. Click **Save**.
4. The notebook will run in the background (takes approximately 2.5 to 3 minutes on Kaggle CPU).

### Step 5: Submit the Output to the Leaderboard
1. Once the run completes, click on the version number (e.g. **Version 1** or the bottom-left notification).
2. Go to the **Output** tab of the notebook.
3. You will see `submission.csv` listed under Output files.
4. Click the blue **"Submit"** (or **"Submit to Competition"**) button next to `submission.csv`.
5. Enter a brief description (e.g., `Grand Multi-GBDT Ensemble (LGBM + XGBoost + CatBoost + RF)`).
6. Click **Submit**!
7. Check the Leaderboard to view your updated public Macro F1 score!
